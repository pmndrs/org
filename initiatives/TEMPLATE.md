# Initiative Name

## Motivations

_Why now? What has changed, what gap or pain exists today, and who feels it? Point to demonstrated problems, not speculative ones (Pragmatism). Two or three short paragraphs or a bullet list is enough._

## Goals

_What does success look like? Start with a one-line definition of done: "A successful completion of this initiative will …" Then list concrete outcomes as bullets. Each should be something you could check off._

## Scope

_One or two sentences on what the initiative covers and which projects/repos it touches._

Included:

- _Work, packages, or deliverables in scope._

Excluded:

- _Adjacent work explicitly out of scope, so reviewers know where the edges are._

## Resources

_What does this need beyond the lead dev? Be honest about the bottleneck. It is often reviewers, docs, demos, or design rather than dev time._

- _Other devs / maintainers: names or "open"._
- _Reviewers: who, and for which phases._
- _Docs, demos, design, outreach: what's needed._
